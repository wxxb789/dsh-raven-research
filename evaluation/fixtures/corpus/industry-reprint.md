# Industry Bulletin: Northbridge adopts seven-year retention

> Controlled Raven benchmark fixture. Fictional secondary coverage.

Publisher: Archive Operations Bulletin
Published: 2026-02-02
Originating record: Northbridge Records Authority final Rule R-17

The Northbridge Records Authority adopted a seven-year retention period for signed operational recovery records.
This bulletin restates the Authority's announcement and adds no independent measurement or reporting.
