# Synthetic Records Retention Rule R-17 (2024 draft)

> Controlled Raven benchmark fixture. Fictional and superseded.

Issuer: Northbridge Records Authority
Issued: 2024-09-10
Status: draft; superseded by the final rule issued 2026-02-01

The draft proposed retaining signed operational recovery records for five years.
It proposed no shorter exception for low-risk training records.
The draft was a consultation proposal and never became effective.
