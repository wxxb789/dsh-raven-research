# Synthetic Independent Recovery Experiment

> Controlled Raven benchmark fixture. Fictional academic-style primary evidence.

Identifier: CIR-60-2026
Publisher: Center for Incident Research
Design: randomized simulated recovery exercise
Sample: 60 teams

Teams assigned centralized record ownership had a median recovery time 12 percent shorter than teams assigned decentralized ownership.
The experiment measured one simulated incident per team and did not measure retention duration, compliance cost, or long-term behavior.
The authors report that the laboratory workflow may not represent production incident pressure.
