# Synthetic Records Retention Rule R-17 (final)

> Controlled Raven benchmark fixture. This document describes fictional institutions and facts; it is not real-world guidance.

Issuer: Northbridge Records Authority
Issued: 2026-02-01
Effective: 2026-07-01
Status: final; supersedes the 2024 draft

Rule R-17 requires signed operational recovery records to be retained for seven years after closure.
Low-risk training records may be deleted after three years when an accountable officer records the exception.
The Authority states that the rule is intended to improve reconstructability after incidents.
The rule contains no measured estimate of recovery speed, failure reduction, adoption, or compliance cost.
